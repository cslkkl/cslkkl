I write plugins, and some things that have nothing to do with efficiency.

Inside DeepSeek Harness I turned two recurring annoyances into plugins. One is CLIProxyAPI account management — balance, check-in, account switching, which used to mean opening its web console and clicking through it. The other is annotating a page in place and asking the Agent directly, which beats a screenshot plus a paragraph of description.

The third one is discography-showcase. Eight artists, 1104 tracks, each artist's albums arranged as concentric year-rings by release year, official 30-second previews, zero dependencies, works offline. It solves nothing. I just wanted to see the shape.

---

### Three projects

**[dsh-cpa-switch](https://github.com/cslkkl/dsh-cpa-switch)** · [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/dsh-cpa-switch)<br>
No more opening the web console for account stuff.

**[dsh-web-annotator](https://github.com/cslkkl/dsh-web-annotator)** · [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/package/dsh-web-annotator)<br>
Point at the page and say "here", faster than screenshotting and describing.

**[discography-showcase](https://github.com/cslkkl/discography-showcase)**<br>
Eight artists, 1104 tracks, ring after ring. Not useful. Good to look at.
